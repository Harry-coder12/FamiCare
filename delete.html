Delete your FamiCare account

This page explains exactly how to delete your FamiCare account and the data associated with it, what will be deleted, what (if anything) is kept, and for how long.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open the FamiCare app and sign in to the account you want deleted.
  2. Go to Settings (the tab at the bottom right).
  3. Tap Data & Privacy.
  4. Under Danger zone, tap Delete all records from this phone. This clears the local copy of the care record immediately.
  5. To delete the account itself as well (email, password, and the server copy of the household record), email us at the address below using the subject line “Delete my account”, from the email address registered to your FamiCare account. We will action the request within 7 days.

Email (account deletion):
famicare.health.app@gmail.com

Include the email address associated with your account. We may ask you to confirm your identity from that same address before proceeding.

What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted

  • Your FamiCare account (email address, password, sign-in identity)
  • Household record and membership
  • People you care for (names, dates of birth, NHS numbers, allergies, GP details)
  • Medicines, dose schedules and dose history
  • Care-log entries and attached photos
  • Vitals and readings
  • Tasks, rota and shift swaps
  • Contacts and the emergency card
  • Documents stored in the vault
  • Expenses and the shared ledger
  • Legal and financial records
  • Care hours
  • Any pending sync data queued on our servers
  • Files in the FamiCare storage bucket that belong to your household

Kept (temporarily)

  • Encrypted backups: a copy of your data may remain in our encrypted backups for up to 30 days after deletion. It is not accessible through the app or our support tools and is removed automatically at the end of that window.
  • Billing records: if you paid for a subscription, Google Play and RevenueCat retain the purchase record for as long as tax law requires. This contains no health or care data.
  • Anonymised, non-identifying error logs: we do not run a crash-reporting or analytics SDK, so in practice there is nothing in this category.
Please note: once the account is deleted, the action cannot be undone. FamiCare does not keep a trash can or a recovery window beyond the encrypted-backup period described above.

Delete some data without deleting your account

If you only want to remove part of your record and keep using FamiCare, you can do all of the following inside the app — no email required, no waiting period:

  • Delete an individual care-log entry, photo, vital, task, contact, document or expense.
  • Delete an entire person and everything attached to them.
  • Export a full copy of your data as JSON before deleting anything (Settings ▸ Data & Privacy ▸ Export everything).
  • Leave a shared household without deleting your account.

The same choices are described in the Google Play Data safety section for FamiCare.

Questions

If anything on this page is unclear, or you would like confirmation that your data has been deleted, email famicare.health.app@gmail.com. A real person reads this inbox.
